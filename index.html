LUMEN

LUMEN is a local content creation, scheduling and publishing application developed by NØVÆ Studio. It helps an authorized user prepare original video content and publish it to the user's own connected social-media accounts.

What LUMEN does

  • Creates and prepares original video content locally.
  • Helps organize titles, descriptions and publication schedules.
  • Connects to supported social platforms only after the user grants permission.
  • Can publish videos on behalf of the authenticated account when the platform grants the required permissions.
  • Can read permitted video information and performance data to help improve future content.

TikTok integration

LUMEN uses TikTok authorization only for features explicitly approved by the account owner. The application does not ask users for their TikTok password and does not sell personal data.

Access can be revoked by disconnecting the TikTok account or removing the application's authorization from the relevant TikTok account settings.
