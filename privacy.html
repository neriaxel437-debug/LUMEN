Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

1. About LUMEN

LUMEN is a local content creation, scheduling and publishing application operated by NØVÆ Studio. It is designed to help an authorized user manage and publish original content to connected social-media accounts.

2. Data we may access

When a user chooses to connect a supported social-media account, LUMEN may request access to account information, video information, publishing permissions and performance statistics necessary for enabled features. The exact data depends on the permissions granted by the platform and user.

3. How data is used

Authorized data is used only to provide requested LUMEN features, including account connection, publishing, scheduling, content management and performance analysis.

4. Local storage

LUMEN is designed primarily as a local application. Authentication tokens and application state may be stored on the user's own device so the user does not have to reconnect at every launch.

5. Data sharing and sale

NØVÆ Studio does not sell personal information obtained through connected social-media accounts.

6. Retention and deletion

Local authorization data may remain on the user's device until the user disconnects the account, removes local application data or revokes platform authorization.

7. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect locally stored credentials and authorization tokens.

8. Children's privacy

LUMEN is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when LUMEN features or platform requirements change.

10. Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to the contact address published by NØVÆ Studio in the developer account associated with LUMEN.
